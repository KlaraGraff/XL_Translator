## 中文

**修复：公式多的 Word 段落，公式仍挤到段末**
10.3.3 起，Word 段落里的公式会留在译文句中原来的位置。但一段里公式很多时（学术论文里常有十几个），只要模型漏掉其中一个的位置，整段的公式就又全部挤到段末。现在译文照常保留，只把漏掉的公式按分句分组（每组最多 5 个，可在「翻译参数 → Word → 每批最多公式数」中调整），请模型补上位置；补不上会按「单段重试次数」重试，必要时拆得更小再试。仍放不回原位的公式放在所在那一句的句末，译文标黄并加批注「公式位置待核对」，方便对照原文调整。特别长的段落（几百字以上）分块翻译时也保留公式位置。

**修复：CAD 图纸用线画的明细表里，英文译文超出格子**
明细表、材料表常用线画出格子，每格放一段多行文字。以前这类文字不会自动缩字，英文折成好几行压到下一行，或伸出表格外框。现在会按格子的宽度换行，仍放不下先把字压窄，再缩小字高；同一列的字号保持一致；AutoCAD 表格里比格子还宽的单词也会缩进格子。缩到最小仍放不下的会列入报告。

## English

**Fix: equations still piled up at the end of equation-heavy Word paragraphs**
Since 10.3.3, equations in Word paragraphs stay where they were in the translated sentence. But when a paragraph held many equations (academic papers often have a dozen or more), losing the position of just one sent all of that paragraph's equations to the end again. Now the translation is kept and only the missed equations are placed: they are grouped by clause (up to 5 per group, adjustable under Translation options → Word → Formulas per batch) and the model is asked for their positions, with retries up to Retries per item, in smaller groups if needed. An equation that still cannot be placed goes at the end of its sentence, which is highlighted in yellow with the comment 「公式位置待核对」 (check equation position) so it can be adjusted against the original. Very long paragraphs translated in several pieces also keep their equation positions.

**Fix: English text overflowing line-drawn schedule cells in CAD drawings**
Parts lists and material schedules are often drawn as line grids with a multi-line text in each cell. This text used to be left as is, so English wrapped into several lines over the next row or ran past the table frame. It now wraps to the cell width and, if it still does not fit, is first narrowed and then reduced in height; text in the same column keeps one size, and words wider than their cell in AutoCAD tables are also shrunk to fit. Text that does not fit at the minimum is listed in the report.
