## 中文

**改进：公式多的 Word 段落，公式大多能留在原位**
10.3.3 起，Word 段落里的公式会留在译文句中原来的位置。但一段里公式很多时（学术论文里常有十几个），只要模型漏掉其中一个的位置，整段的公式就又全部挤到段末。现在译文照常保留，只把漏掉的公式按分句分组（每组最多 5 个，可在「翻译参数 → Word → 每批最多公式数」中调整），请模型补上位置；补不上会按「单段重试次数」重试，必要时拆得更小再试。特别长的段落（几百字以上）分块翻译时也保留公式位置。个别公式多次重试后仍可能放不回原位，这时放在所在那一句的句末，译文标黄并加批注「公式位置待核对」，方便对照原文调整。

**改进：PDF 图纸里画成线条的中文、日文也能识别并翻译**
有些图纸导出成 PDF 后，文字是用线条画出来的，没有文字层。以前「文字识别组件」只能识别英文、法文等西方文字，这类中文、日文图纸无法翻译。现在文字识别组件换成了新的识别模型，中文、日文和西方文字都能识别，画得很淡、很小的字也能识别，译文放回原位；原文语言为「自动识别」时，也会结合识别出的文字判断原文语言。请在「扩展 → 组件」中安装或更新「文字识别组件」（需先安装「PDF 处理组件」）。识别结果不能保证完全准确，个别字仍可能认错或漏掉，重要图纸请对照原文核对。韩文暂不支持。

**修复：PDF 结果提示把未翻译的文件都说成 A3+ 页面**
以前整份未翻译的 PDF 一律提示为「全部为 A3+ 页面」，A3 图纸识别失败也这样说。现在只有因幅面跳过的文件才这样提示，其余提示未翻译的文件数。

**改进：CAD 图纸用线画的明细表里，英文译文不再轻易超出格子**
明细表、材料表常用线画出格子，每格放一段多行文字。以前这类文字不会自动缩字，英文折成好几行压到下一行，或伸出表格外框。现在会按格子的宽度换行，仍放不下先把字压窄，再缩小字高；同一列的字号保持一致；AutoCAD 表格里比格子还宽的单词也会缩进格子。格子特别窄时，缩到最小比例仍可能放不下，这些位置会列入报告，方便复核。

**改进：界面细节更精致**
分隔线和卡片边框调淡，输入框选中时的高亮更柔和，深色主题的文字粗细更协调，数字等宽对齐；运行日志的新内容淡入显示；复制成功后按钮上显示「✓ 已复制」。右栏「高级」展开、收起更利落，右栏不再出现左右滚动。

## English

**Improvement: equations in equation-heavy Word paragraphs now mostly stay in place**
Since 10.3.3, equations in Word paragraphs stay where they were in the translated sentence. But when a paragraph held many equations (academic papers often have a dozen or more), losing the position of just one sent all of that paragraph's equations to the end again. Now the translation is kept and only the missed equations are placed: they are grouped by clause (up to 5 per group, adjustable under Translation options → Word → Formulas per batch) and the model is asked for their positions, with retries up to Retries per item, in smaller groups if needed. Very long paragraphs translated in several pieces also keep their equation positions. An occasional equation may still not be placed after all retries; it then goes at the end of its sentence, which is highlighted in yellow with the comment 「公式位置待核对」 (check equation position) so it can be adjusted against the original.

**Improvement: Chinese and Japanese text drawn as lines in PDF drawings can now be recognized and translated**
In some drawings exported to PDF, the text is drawn as lines and has no text layer. The Text recognition component used to read only Western scripts such as English and French, so such Chinese and Japanese drawings could not be translated. It now uses a new recognition model that reads Chinese, Japanese and Western scripts, including very faint and very small text, and the translation is put back in place; with the source language set to Auto-detect, the recognized text is also used to determine the source language. Install or update the Text recognition component under Extensions → Components (the PDF processing component is required first). Recognition is not guaranteed to be perfect: an occasional character may still be misread or missed, so please check important drawings against the original. Korean is not supported yet.

**Fix: PDF result notice describing every untranslated file as A3+ pages**
Every PDF left entirely untranslated used to be reported as "all pages are A3+", even A3 drawings where recognition failed. Only files skipped because of their page size are now reported this way; for the others, the notice gives the number of untranslated files.

**Improvement: English text in line-drawn schedule cells in CAD drawings now rarely overflows**
Parts lists and material schedules are often drawn as line grids with a multi-line text in each cell. This text used to be left as is, so English wrapped into several lines over the next row or ran past the table frame. It now wraps to the cell width and, if it still does not fit, is first narrowed and then reduced in height; text in the same column keeps one size, and words wider than their cell in AutoCAD tables are also shrunk to fit. In very narrow cells, text may still not fit at the minimum scale; those places are listed in the report for review.

**Improvement: a more refined interface**
Dividers and card borders are lighter, the highlight on a focused input is softer, text weights in the dark theme are better balanced, and numbers line up at equal width; new run log lines fade in; a successful copy shows "✓ Copied" on the button. Advanced in the right panel opens and closes more crisply, and the right panel no longer scrolls sideways.
