## 中文

**修复：公式多的 Word 段落，公式仍挤到段末**
10.3.3 起，Word 段落里的公式会留在译文句中原来的位置。但一段里公式很多时（学术论文里常有十几个），只要模型漏掉其中一个的位置，整段的公式就又全部挤到段末；一段超过 16 个公式时也不处理。现在遇到这种情况，会让模型把这一段单独重新翻一次，仍不完整时按句子拆成两半再翻；万一还漏，就按公式在原文里的前后位置放回，其余公式照常留在原处。一段最多可处理 40 个公式。另外，特别长的段落（几百字以上）以前分块翻译时公式位置会丢，现在每块都带着公式位置，翻完按原位拼回。

**修复：CAD 图纸用线画的明细表里，英文译文超出格子**
明细表、材料表常用线画出格子，每格放一段多行文字。以前这类文字不会自动缩字，英文折成好几行压到下一行，或伸出表格外框。现在会按格子的宽度换行，仍放不下先把字压窄，再缩小字高；同一列的字号保持一致；AutoCAD 表格里比格子还宽的单词也会缩进格子。缩到最小仍放不下的会列入报告。

## English

**Fix: equations still piled up at the end of equation-heavy Word paragraphs**
Since 10.3.3, equations in Word paragraphs stay where they were in the translated sentence. But when a paragraph held many equations (academic papers often have a dozen or more), losing the position of just one sent all of that paragraph's equations to the end again, and paragraphs with more than 16 equations were not handled at all. Now the model translates that paragraph again on its own, and if it is still incomplete, in two halves split at a sentence end; if an equation is still missed, it is put back according to its neighbours in the original while the others stay in place. A paragraph can hold up to 40 equations. Very long paragraphs, which are translated in several pieces, used to lose the equation positions; each piece now keeps them and the pieces are joined back with the equations in place.

**Fix: English text overflowing line-drawn schedule cells in CAD drawings**
Parts lists and material schedules are often drawn as line grids with a multi-line text in each cell. This text used to be left as is, so English wrapped into several lines over the next row or ran past the table frame. It now wraps to the cell width and, if it still does not fit, is first narrowed and then reduced in height; text in the same column keeps one size, and words wider than their cell in AutoCAD tables are also shrunk to fit. Text that does not fit at the minimum is listed in the report.
