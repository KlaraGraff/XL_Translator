## 中文

**改进：公式多的 Word 论文翻译效果更好**
针对论文等包含大量公式的 Word 文档做了优化，公式能更准确地留在译文中原来的位置。个别放不回原位的公式会放在句末，译文标黄并加批注「公式位置待核对」。

**改进：PDF 图纸里画成线条的中文、日文也能识别**
PDF 用「原位替换文字」翻译时，画成线条的中文、日文（包括很淡、很小的字）现在也能识别并翻译。请在「扩展 → 组件」中安装或更新「文字识别组件」。

**改进：CAD 明细表里的英文译文不再超出格子**
用线画的明细表、材料表里，译文按格子宽度换行，放不下时自动压窄、缩小，同一列字号保持一致。

**修复：PDF 结果提示把未翻译的文件都说成 A3+ 页面**
现在只有因幅面跳过的文件才这样提示。

**改进：界面细节更精致**
线条和高亮更柔和，复制后显示「✓ 已复制」；右栏「高级」展开更利落，不再左右滚动。

## English

**Improvement: better translation of equation-heavy Word papers**
Word documents with many equations, such as academic papers, have been optimized: equations stay more reliably in their original place in the translation. An occasional equation that cannot be placed goes at the end of its sentence, which is highlighted in yellow with the comment 「公式位置待核对」 (check equation position).

**Improvement: Chinese and Japanese text drawn as lines in PDF drawings is now recognized**
With "Replace text in place", Chinese and Japanese text drawn as lines in PDF drawings (including very faint and very small text) is now recognized and translated. Install or update the Text recognition component under Extensions → Components.

**Improvement: English text no longer overflows CAD schedule cells**
In parts lists and material schedules drawn as line grids, translations wrap to the cell width, are narrowed and shrunk when they do not fit, and keep one size per column.

**Fix: PDF result notice describing every untranslated file as A3+ pages**
Only files skipped because of their page size are now reported this way.

**Improvement: a more refined interface**
Lines and highlights are softer, and a successful copy shows "✓ Copied"; Advanced in the right panel opens more crisply and no longer scrolls sideways.
