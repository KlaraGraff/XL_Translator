## 中文

**修复：公式多的 Word 段落，公式仍挤到段末**
10.3.3 起，Word 段落里的公式会留在译文句中原来的位置。但一段里公式很多时（学术论文里常有十几个），只要模型漏掉其中一个的位置，整段的公式就又全部挤到段末；一段超过 16 个公式时也不处理。现在遇到这种情况，会让模型把这一段单独重新翻一次，仍不完整时按句子拆成两半再翻；万一还漏，就按公式在原文里的前后位置放回，其余公式照常留在原处。一段最多可处理 40 个公式。

## English

**Fix: equations still piled up at the end of equation-heavy Word paragraphs**
Since 10.3.3, equations in Word paragraphs stay where they were in the translated sentence. But when a paragraph held many equations (academic papers often have a dozen or more), losing the position of just one sent all of that paragraph's equations to the end again, and paragraphs with more than 16 equations were not handled at all. Now the model translates that paragraph again on its own, and if it is still incomplete, in two halves split at a sentence end; if an equation is still missed, it is put back according to its neighbours in the original while the others stay in place. A paragraph can hold up to 40 equations.
