## 中文

**改进：含大量公式的 Word 文档翻译**
针对学术论文等含大量公式的 Word 文档进行了优化，翻译后公式能更准确地保留在原有位置。少数无法确定位置的公式将置于所在句末，并以黄色底色和批注「公式位置待核对」标示，便于人工核对。

**改进：PDF 图纸可识别以线条绘制的中文、日文**
部分 PDF 图纸中的文字是以线条图形绘制的，无法直接提取文本。使用「原位替换文字」翻译方式时，现可识别此类中文、日文文字（包括颜色较浅、字号较小的文字）并完成翻译。使用前请在「扩展 → 组件」中安装或更新「文字识别组件」。

**改进：CAD 明细表译文自动适应单元格**
对于以线条绘制的明细表、材料表，译文按单元格宽度自动换行；仍超出时自动缩小字宽和字高，同一列保持统一字号，避免译文超出表格。

**修复：PDF 翻译结果提示不准确**
此前，未翻译的 PDF 文件均被提示为「全部为 A3+ 页面」。现仅在文件因页面尺寸过大而被跳过时显示该提示。

**改进：使用体验**
优化了界面视觉效果和使用体验。

## English

**Improvement: translating Word documents with many equations**
Word documents with many equations, such as academic papers, have been optimized: equations now stay more reliably in their original positions after translation. The few equations whose position cannot be determined are placed at the end of their sentence, marked with a yellow highlight and the comment 「公式位置待核对」 (check equation position) for manual review.

**Improvement: Chinese and Japanese text drawn as lines in PDF drawings can be recognized**
In some PDF drawings, text is drawn as line graphics and cannot be extracted directly. With the "Replace text in place" method, such Chinese and Japanese text (including light-coloured and small text) can now be recognized and translated. Before use, install or update the Text recognition component under Extensions → Components.

**Improvement: CAD schedule translations fit their cells**
In parts lists and material schedules drawn with lines, translations wrap to the cell width; if they still do not fit, the character width and height are reduced automatically, with one text size per column, so the translation stays within the table.

**Fix: inaccurate PDF result notice**
Previously, every untranslated PDF file was reported as "all pages are A3+". This notice now appears only when a file is skipped because its pages are too large.

**Improvement: user experience**
The interface visuals and overall user experience have been improved.
